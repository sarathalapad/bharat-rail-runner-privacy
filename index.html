Bharat Rail Runner Privacy Policy

SarathKumar.K · Effective date: 26 October 2026 (updated)

Summary

Bharat Rail Runner is an endless-runner game. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play. The game does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. Your game progress is saved on your device. We do not sell or rent any data. There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no way for players to talk to each other.

1. Who we are

Bharat Rail Runner (Android package com.bharat.railrunner) is published by SarathKumar.K ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following on your device only, so that you can continue where you left off: best scores and distance, coins and keys, unlocked characters and boosters, the weekly league table (calculated on your device), tutorial progress, chosen language and settings (sound, music, vibration). This data does not leave your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to play. To check that a connection is available, it sends a simple request to a Google connectivity address (clients3.google.com/generate_204). This request contains no personal information from the game; like any internet request, it reveals your device's IP address to that server.

4. Advertising

Bharat Rail Runner is free. It may show ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK by Google LLC): a full-screen ad when the game starts, a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between runs, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses the Android Advertising ID (or similar device identifiers) to deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, the ad SDK may automatically process technical information such as IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device model, operating system version, language, app version and ad interaction events. This processing is done by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Children

Bharat Rail Runner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices

You can delete all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. You can reset your device's ad settings in your Android or Google account settings.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
