Privacy Policy - Bharat Rail Runner

Developer: Sarathkumar.K · App package: com.bharat.railrunner
Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Contact: alapadsarath@gmail.com

This policy explains how the Android game Bharat Rail Runner ("the game", "we") handles information. In short: the game has no accounts, we run no servers for it, and your game data stays on your device. The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described below. An internet connection is required to play.

1. Data stored on your device

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any login.

Your progress and preferences (coins, keys, high scores, unlocked characters and boards, missions, daily rewards, your weekly league position, language, settings, and your age band) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. The weekly league is calculated on your device against computer-controlled rivals: no other player sees your score. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include the game's files in your own personal Google backup; we have no access to that backup.

2. Age screen and age bands

On first launch the game asks for your birth year on a neutral screen, before any ad is requested and before any consent form. The year is used once, on your device, to choose one of three age bands: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over. Because only the year is asked, the younger possible age is used. The birth year itself is not saved; only the band is kept on your device, and it decides how ads work (see section 3). It can only be answered again after Reset Profile in Settings or after clearing the game's data. An age that has not been answered is treated like under 13, and no ad is requested until the screen is answered.

We never receive your birth year or band. Ad requests sent to Google carry only the resulting settings shown in the table below, such as the child-directed tag and the ad content limit.

Ads are served only through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. No other ad network or mediation is used. The game uses four ad formats:

4. Consent

Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and US states with applicable privacy laws), players aged 18 and over see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can accept, reject, or manage your choices. Where required, a Privacy Options button appears in the game's Settings so you can change your choice at any time.

5. Other network use

The game needs the internet because it is funded by ads. While it runs, it regularly checks the connection by contacting a Google connectivity address (clients3.google.com). This check sends no game data, though your IP address is visible to Google as with any internet request.

The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send anonymous device statistics (such as device model and operating system version) to Unity Technologies. See the Unity Privacy Policy. The game has no analytics of its own, no cloud save and no online leaderboard.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. For a player who enters an age under 13:

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, are non-personalised, and are limited to general-audience content.
  • App Open and banner ads are not shown. Interstitial and rewarded ads are limited to lower daily caps with longer gaps.
  • No consent form is shown, and the game asks for no personal information.
  • An adult check is required before leaving the game for a web page, such as this policy, and before resetting the profile.

If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com and we will help you resolve it.

7. In-app purchases

The game currently has no real-money purchases. Coins and keys are earned in the game.

8. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset Profile in the game, by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Bharat Rail Runner > Storage > Clear data), or by uninstalling the game. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Questions about deletion can be sent to alapadsarath@gmail.com.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

9. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
